# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Georgian school students (children and teenagers) learning English vocabulary, mostly on phones and on home or school computers. Adults and people learning programming English use it too, but students come first.

## Product Purpose

English Quest (ისწავლე ინგლისური, www.englishquest.space) teaches English words to Georgian speakers. The learner types in (or picks) a list of words and drills it in three steps: copy the word shown (mistakes turn red), write it from memory, then write every word and press „ჩაბარება“ to see what was right and what was wrong. Success means students come back every day and actually remember the words.

## Positioning

Free (the only promotion is a small footer banner for the partner CodeQuest), entirely in Georgian, and built around the learner's own word lists: the copy → write → submit drill works on any list, alongside ready-made CEFR level lists (A1–C2), topic packs and a programming-English course.

## Operating Context

- Used in the browser on phones and computers; installable as a Windows or phone app with an offline copy.
- Accounts by email/password or Google (Supabase); progress syncs across devices, with local profiles as a fallback.
- Learners switch the keyboard between Georgian and English while typing answers; English pronunciation comes from the browser's voice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-file static app `index.html` (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no build step) hosted on Vercel, Georgian UI. Public pages (course, levels, words, about) are generated by `build-pages.mjs` from the app's own data and styled by `site.css`.
- Study modes: cards, copy, write, test („ჩაბარება“), quiz, dictation. XP, day streak, daily goal, statistics, hard-word review, progress saved per lesson. Profile with avatar, CEFR level and badges. Settings with style choice, voice speed, auto speak, export and import.
- Saved data (localStorage and the Supabase `user_data` JSON) must stay readable by the new version.
- This round's goals from the user: faster loading, better on phones, simpler to use.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: English Quest / ისწავლე ინგლისური; footer "Powered by codequest" and the CodeQuest partner banner (codequest.space, own logo colours, labelled პარტნიორი).
- Logo stays: red square with a white open book and a red bookmark.
- The logo red stays as the main accent; the rest of the palette is free (2026-11 redesign: study-notes notebook, white dot-grid paper, gel-pen ink, pastel highlighters).
- The 8 selectable styles stay as notebook variants. Stored ids stay for saved data: paper is the default notebook, then auto, light, dark, terminal, ocean, forest, rose.
- One line-icon set replaces emojis in the interface (small in buttons, large on cards). Emojis remain only as stickers: avatars and names people type.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content in `index.html`: 240 programming words in 12 modules, 120 CEFR level words, 6 topic packs. Logo files and site screenshots in `ფოტოები/` (not deployed). No testimonials, user numbers, ratings or press exist; never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The drill is the product: reaching the next word should take the fewest taps.
2. Georgian first, written for school students: short, plain, encouraging words.
3. Fast on a cheap phone over a slow connection.
4. Free and honest: no ads, no invented numbers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

School-age users; Georgian script throughout; keyboard and touch are both first-class; respect reduced motion.
