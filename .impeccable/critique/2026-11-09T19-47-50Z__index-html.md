## Overall Impression

The home screen is the best it has been: one sticky note, one red button, then your lessons. The weakest moment is the end of a study session. A student who scores 2/10 lands on a wall of pink form errors, a ticking timer and leftover flip tiles. The biggest opportunity is to make the test result a teacher-marked notebook page.

## What's Working

1. **Home screen hierarchy:** sticky note with the next word, a single red continue, lessons with highlighter icon circles and progress strokes. The next word is one tap away, matching "the drill is the product".
2. **Copy-step letter cells:** each letter in its own cell, green highlighter for right, red pen for wrong, instant Georgian-keyboard detection, autocorrect and capitals off.
3. **Notebook objects with restraint:** lined index flashcard, ruled lesson textarea, tape strip. Dark and kraft styles keep the same structure.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The test result reads as form errors, not a corrected page**
- Why it matters: it is the end of every session and what students remember (peak-end). After a bad score it feels like failing a form; the split-flap score breaks the notebook world.
- Fix: score at the top in red ink inside a hand-drawn circle with one encouraging line; strike through each wrong answer and write the correct word beside it in red pen; green highlighter on right ones; stop and hide the timer after submit; drop the flip-tile score style.
- Suggested command: /impeccable delight, then /impeccable polish

**[P1] Red means too many things**
- Why it matters: red is meant to be the one main action, but it also marks "don't know" and "delete everything", while the real main actions (submit test, create lesson) are dark ink. Students learn red = go, then press it to delete.
- Fix: submit test and create lesson in red; "don't know" neutral; "delete everything" as an outlined red-ink button behind its confirm.
- Suggested command: /impeccable colorize

**[P1] The drill is not focused on phones**
- Why it matters: this is the core loop on cheap Android phones. The partner card and footer sit under the drill; the 6-mode bar scrolls sideways and hides "2. write" and "3. submit" at 375px; the test's only submit button sits below 10 stacked inputs with the keyboard open.
- Fix: hide partner banner and footer inside study modes; replace the mode bar with a 3-step track (1 copy · 2 write · 3 submit) and put cards, quiz and dictation under "more"; keep the write field and its button above the keyboard; set enterkeyhint="next"/"done" on test inputs.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt (with /impeccable distill for the mode bar)

**[P2] In copy mode a wrong letter erases the letter to copy**
- Why it matters: the student loses the model letter exactly when they need it.
- Fix: keep the target letter visible in or above the red cell, or hold the cursor until the letter is right.
- Suggested command: /impeccable clarify

**[P2] Accessibility gaps in the drill**
- Why it matters: screen readers read English words with the Georgian voice, and test fields have no names.
- Fix: aria-labelledby from each test input to its prompt; lang="en" on English words; strip emoji from aria-labels; "⋯" to at least 44×44; an h1 in the signed-in app; aria-current on the active mode.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden
